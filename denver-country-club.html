Modern Technology for Historic Homes

Denver's Country Club neighborhood has fewer than 400 homes, yet it holds some of the city's most architecturally significant residences. Developed in the early 1900s alongside the Denver Country Club, its tree-lined blocks between Downing Street and University Boulevard are filled with Tudor, Colonial and Mediterranean-style homes on generous lots, much of it inside the Country Club Historic District.

Homes like these deserve technology that is powerful and nearly invisible. Wired Up Solutions plans every install around the architecture: hidden speakers, concealed wiring, low-profile cameras and controls that look at home on a century-old wall.

Careful Retrofits for Historic Properties

Adding technology to a 100-year-old home takes a different approach than a new build. Here's how we work:

  • Minimal wall openings: We fish cable through attics, basements and existing chases, and use wireless equipment where that's the smarter choice.
  • Respect for the exterior: Cameras, doorbells and outdoor gear are chosen and placed to be discreet. For exterior work in the historic district, we plan with Denver's design guidelines in mind.
  • Renovation-friendly: If walls are already open, we prewire during the project and coordinate with your architect and builder.
  • Tidy infrastructure: Equipment lives in a clean, labeled rack in a basement or closet, out of sight but easy to service.
