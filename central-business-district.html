Technology Infrastructure for Downtown Businesses

Denver's Central Business District, also branded Upper Downtown, is the city's commercial core. Bordered by Speer Boulevard, Lawrence Street, 20th Street, Broadway and Colfax Avenue, it's home to office towers along 17th Street, the Colorado Convention Center, the Denver Performing Arts Complex, and a dense mix of hotels, restaurants and retail.

Businesses here depend on networks that never drop, conference rooms that work the first time, and security that protects people and property in a busy urban setting. Wired Up Solutions designs, installs and supports the low-voltage systems behind all of it, working on site throughout downtown.

Built Around Downtown Buildings

Working in high-rise and historic commercial buildings takes coordination. We plan for:

  • Building management: We follow building rules for access, work hours and cable pathways, and coordinate with property managers and landlords.
  • Tenant build-outs: We cable during construction so data drops, camera locations, access-controlled doors and AV are ready on move-in day.
  • Existing infrastructure: Inherited a suite full of unlabeled cable? We assess it, reuse what's sound, and clean up or replace the rest.
  • Minimal disruption: We plan installs around your operations, including phased work, and can discuss after-hours scheduling.
