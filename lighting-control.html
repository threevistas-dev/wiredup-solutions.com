General Questions

A lighting control system is a smart solution that allows you to manage and automate your lighting from a centralized system. Using smart lighting controls, you can turn lights on/off, dim them, schedule them, or control them remotely.

Installing a smart lighting control system offers:

  • Improved energy efficiency
  • Enhanced convenience and automation
  • Custom lighting scenes and ambiance
  • Remote access and control
  • Increased security and safety

Types of Lighting Control Systems

We install a wide range of lighting automation systems, including:

  • Smart light switches and dimmers
  • Wireless lighting control systems
  • Centralized lighting control panels
  • Occupancy and motion sensor lighting
  • Outdoor and landscape lighting control systems

These systems are ideal for both residential lighting control and commercial lighting automation.

The main differences are:

  • Smart lighting systems allow remote control, automation, and integration.
  • Traditional lighting requires manual operation with limited flexibility
