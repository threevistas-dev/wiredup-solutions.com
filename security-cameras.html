General Questions

A security camera system (also known as a CCTV system) is a video surveillance solution designed to monitor and record activity at your home or business. These systems help deter crime, improve safety, and provide recorded footage for review.

Installing a home or business security camera system provides:

  • Crime deterrence and theft prevention
  • 24/7 video surveillance
  • Remote viewing from your smartphone
  • Recorded evidence in case of incidents
  • Increased property security and peace of mind

Types of Security Camera Systems

We install a wide range of security camera systems, including:

  • IP security cameras (network cameras)
  • HD CCTV cameras
  • Wireless security camera systems
  • Outdoor security cameras
  • Indoor surveillance cameras
  • Night vision and motion detection cameras

These solutions are ideal for both residential security camera installation and commercial CCTV systems.

  • IP cameras connect through the internet and offer higher resolution and remote access
  • CCTV (analog) cameras use coaxial cables and are typically more budget-friendly
