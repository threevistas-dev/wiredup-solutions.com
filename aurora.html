Smart Technology Across Colorado's Third-Largest City

Aurora stretches across Arapahoe, Adams and Douglas counties, with hundreds of neighborhoods and nearly every type of home. Established areas near East Colfax and central Aurora are full of ranch and mid-century homes, while southeast Aurora has newer master-planned communities like Tallyn's Reach, Saddle Rock and the neighborhoods around Southlands. The city is also home to major employers, including the CU Anschutz Medical Campus and Buckley Space Force Base, and continues to grow around the Fitzsimons Innovation Community.

Wired Up Solutions serves all of it, upgrading older homes, prewiring new ones, and installing cameras, access control and networks for Aurora businesses.

Built for Aurora's Mix of Homes and Businesses

  • New construction: Builder packages often include little low-voltage wiring. We prewire before drywall for speakers, cameras, access points and TVs so you're not retrofitting later.
  • Established homes: Ranch and mid-century homes in central and north Aurora rarely have network wiring. We combine attic and basement runs with wireless gear to modernize without major demolition.
  • Home offices: Wired connections and dedicated access points for reliable video calls and remote work.
  • Businesses: Cameras, access control, structured cabling and Wi-Fi for retail along Colfax and Havana, medical and professional offices, and growing businesses around Fitzsimons.
