General Questions

An access control system is a security solution that manages and restricts entry to your property. Instead of traditional keys, it uses keyless entry systems like keycards, PIN codes, mobile apps, or biometrics to control access.

Installing a door access control system for your home or business provides:

  • Enhanced building security
  • Keyless convenience
  • Control over who enters your property
  • Real-time access control monitoring
  • Easy credential management

Types of Access Control Systems

We install a wide range of electronic access control systems, including:

  • Keycard and fob access systems
  • Keypad door entry systems
  • Mobile access control systems
  • Biometric access control systems (fingerprint & facial recognition)
  • Smart lock and keyless entry systems
  • Video intercom and door entry systems

These are ideal for both residential access control systems and commercial access control solutions.

The best access control system installation depends on your needs:

  • Keycard or keypad systems for offices
  • Cloud-based access control systems for multi-door or large facilities
