Smart Living in the Heart of the City

Downtown Denver living means historic warehouse lofts in LoDo, the city's oldest neighborhood, and glass high-rise condos around Union Station, all a short walk from Coors Field, Ball Arena, Larimer Square and Riverfront Park. Designed for Lofts and High-Rises

Downtown homes come with their own challenges. Here's what we plan for:

  • Exposed brick and concrete: When there's no drywall to hide wire behind, we use careful routing, low-profile raceway and wireless devices to keep the look clean.
  • HOA and building rules: We review your building's guidelines first and coordinate with management on hallway-facing hardware, access and work hours.
  • Large windows: Motorized shades on schedules cut glare and heat, and they can close automatically while you're away.
  • Shared walls: We aim speakers and set up home theater systems to deliver great sound without disturbing the neighbors.
