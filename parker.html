Smart Technology for Parker Families

Parker blends small-town character with big-city access. The historic Mainstreet district, which grew up around the Twenty Mile House stagecoach stop, anchors a town of mostly single-family homes in planned communities like The Pinery, Stonegate and Stroh Ranch, with golf-course homes at Pradera and horse properties on the town's edges. Many residents commute to the Denver Tech Center or work from home.

Wired Up Solutions designs systems around how Parker households actually live: reliable Wi-Fi for remote work and school, theaters and media rooms for movie night, and security and smart locks that keep busy families safe and connected.
