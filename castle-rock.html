Smart Homes for a Fast-Growing Town

Castle Rock, the Douglas County seat about 30 miles south of downtown Denver on I-25, has been one of the Front Range's fastest-growing communities. Its population grew roughly 17% between 2020 and early 2025, and new neighborhoods keep arriving, from The Meadows and Crystal Valley Ranch to Terrain, Castlewood Ranch and Dawson Trails. Established areas like Red Hawk Ridge, Bell Mountain Ranch and the historic downtown add to the mix.

With so much new construction, timing is everything. Wired Up Solutions helps Castle Rock homeowners prewire during the build, upgrade builder-grade technology after closing, and bring older homes up to date.

Getting the Timing Right on New Construction

Much of Castle Rock's housing is new or nearly new, and builder packages usually include only basic wiring. Here's how we help:

  • Before drywall: We prewire for speakers, cameras, TVs, access points and touch panels while walls are open. It's the cleanest and most cost-effective time.
  • After closing: Missed the prewire window? We can still add most features with careful attic, basement and wireless solutions.
  • Builder-grade upgrades: We replace basic routers and doorbells with systems designed for the size of your home.
  • Acreage and rural properties: Outbuildings, long drives and gates get dedicated cabling, cameras and Wi-Fi.
