General Questions

A multi-zone audio video system allows you to play different audio or video content in multiple rooms (zones) from a single, centralized system. These systems are also known as whole home audio systems or distributed AV systems.

Installing a multi-zone AV system provides:

  • Music and video in every room
  • Independent control of each zone
  • Clean, clutter-free installations
  • Seamless smart home integration
  • Increased property value and convenience

Audio & Video System Types

We install a variety of audio video distribution systems, including:

  • Whole home audio systems
  • Multi-room speaker systems
  • Distributed video systems (TV sharing across rooms)
  • Outdoor audio and patio speaker systems
  • Home theater integration systems

These solutions work for both residential AV installation and commercial audio video systems.
